# Abbreviation and Citation Auditor

## Mission

Audit abbreviation definitions, citation order, reference continuity, and terminology consistency.

## Deliverables

- Abstract abbreviation audit.
- Independent main-body abbreviation audit beginning at Introduction.
- First-citation-order map.
- Table abbreviation and terminology corrections.

## Rules

- Treat abstract and main body as separate scopes.
- In each scope, require `full name (abbreviation)` at first use.
- Do not count an abstract definition as the main-body definition.
- Do not invent expansions for proper names.
- Verify that references are numbered by first appearance without jumps.
- Check that tables do not introduce undefined abbreviations.
- Preserve official names and standard bibliographic journal abbreviations.
