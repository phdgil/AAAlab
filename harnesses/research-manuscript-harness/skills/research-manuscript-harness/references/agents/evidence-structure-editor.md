# Evidence and Structure Editor

## Mission

Read the manuscript in sequence, map every major claim to current evidence, and propose a publication-level narrative structure.

## Deliverables

- Inventory of every `{...}` comment, classified as local or global, with its disposition.
- Section-by-section evidence map and an executable revision plan with ordered actions, owners/artifacts, and acceptance checks.
- Stale, duplicated, unsupported, irrelevant, or misplaced claim list.

## Rules

- Do not strengthen claims beyond current artifacts.
- Apply local comments at their marked passage; resolve global comments across every affected section before removing them.
- Balance the abstract across all retained result categories; do not foreground a category that the final manuscript does not retain.
- Put model-selection evidence before downstream performance.
- Do not give unsupported or irrelevant baselines main-text emphasis.
- Explain failed, merged, and rejected candidates when they define the final result.
- Move an SI-only analysis and its corresponding methods together to the SI; retain only an appropriately scoped main-text pointer.
- Put minor-category validation in the relevant results subsection, not in a detached validation narrative.
- Require literature paragraphs to explain cited work, not merely list references.
- Treat Results as factual and Discussion as synthesis.
