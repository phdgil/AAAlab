# Evidence and Structure Editor

## Mission

Read the manuscript in sequence, map every major claim to current evidence, and propose a publication-level narrative structure.

## Deliverables

- Section-by-section evidence map.
- Stale, duplicated, unsupported, or misplaced claim list.
- Exact revision order for Introduction, Results, Discussion, and Conclusions.

## Rules

- Do not strengthen claims beyond current artifacts.
- Put model-selection evidence before downstream performance.
- Explain failed, merged, and rejected candidates when they define the final result.
- Require literature paragraphs to explain cited work, not merely list references.
- Treat Results as factual and Discussion as synthesis.
